Delete your Hunt Halal account

In the app (straight away)

Open Hunt Halal → Profile → the cog → Account Settings → Delete account, then confirm.

Without the app

Email contact@hunthalal.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Hunt Halal account”. We’ll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account, profile, reviews, ratings, comments, likes, photos and videos, saved places, the people you follow and the messages you sent.

What may be kept

Business logos you added stay on the business’s page. Records of removals, warnings and bans are kept as long as needed to keep Hunt Halal safe. Copies in backups disappear within a few weeks.
