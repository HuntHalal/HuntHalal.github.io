How many halal places are there in London?

Hunt Halal lists 1,834 halal places in London: 249 restaurants, 785 takeaways, 41 cafés, 49 dessert spots, 5 food trucks and 705 halal butchers. New places are added as people suggest them.

Are these places in London certified halal?

Not necessarily. The places on this page were found through OpenStreetMap halal tags, halal chains and business names, and have not yet been checked by Hunt Halal. In the app every listing shows a badge (Certified, Owner confirmed, Community reported or Unverified) so you can see how its halal status is known.
