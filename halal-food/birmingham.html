Questions about halal food in Birmingham

How many halal places are there in Birmingham?

Hunt Halal lists 379 halal places in Birmingham: 70 restaurants, 124 takeaways, 10 cafés, 8 dessert spots, 2 food trucks and 165 halal butchers. New places are added as people suggest them.

Are these places in Birmingham certified halal?

Not necessarily. The places on this page were found through OpenStreetMap halal tags, halal chains and business names, and have not yet been checked by Hunt Halal. In the app every listing shows a badge (Certified, Owner confirmed, Community reported or Unverified) so you can see how its halal status is known. Always check with the business if it matters to you.

Where can I find a halal butcher in Birmingham?

There are 165 halal butchers listed in Birmingham. See the halal butchers section on this page, or open the map in the Hunt Halal app.
