Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Who we are

Hunt Halal is an app for finding and reviewing halal food in the UK. It is run by DTA Digital Studio Ltd ("we", "us"), a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17585395), registered office The Globe Centre, St James Square, Accrington, BB5 0RE. We are responsible for your personal information under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Questions or requests about your information: contact@hunthalal.com. Post: DTA Digital Studio Ltd, The Globe Centre, St James Square, Accrington, BB5 0RE.

What we collect

Your account: your email address and a password (stored scrambled, we can't read it), or, if you continue with Apple or Google, the name and email address they share with us (Apple lets you hide your real email). Also your display name and, if you add one, a profile photo.

Your age group: whether you're 13 to 17 or 18 and over. We ask once, so under-18s get safer settings. We don't ask for your date of birth.

What you post: reviews, star ratings, dishes you name, halal checks you share, photos of halal certificates and menus, comments, likes, photos and videos, lists of places you make (and notes on them), places you suggest and details you suggest for places.

Private messages you send and receive, so they can be delivered. Your messages are private: we only read a message if someone reports it to us, or if the law requires us to.

Your activity in the app: who you follow, your favourites, the foods you tell us you like, and the places you open, so we can recommend places you'll like.

Usage data: which screens you open, what you search for (and whether it found anything), places you open, and actions like posting a review, liking, sharing or tapping directions, with your phone type and app version. It's linked to a random ID made up by the app, not to your account, name or email, and it never leaves our own database. We tell you about it the first time you open the app, and nothing is saved or sent before then. It starts switched off for under-18s. You can switch it off any time in Settings → Share usage data.

Crash reports: if the app goes wrong on your phone, we receive what went wrong (the error message, where in the app's code it happened, which screen you were on, your phone type and app version), so we can fix it. Your name, email, posts and account are never sent with it. If Share usage data is on, it carries the same random ID as usage data. Crash reports are deleted after 90 days.

Feedback and appeals you choose to send us (Settings → Send feedback, the occasional "Would you recommend Hunt Halal?" question, "How easy was that?" after posting a review, and appeals against our decisions): what you write, your answer, which screen you were on and your phone type. If you're logged in it's linked to your account, and if you tick "You can email me" (or send an appeal) we may reply by email. Only the Hunt Halal team can read it, and it's deleted with your account.

Reports you make, and any warnings, removals or suspensions on your account.

If you claim a business: your name, phone number, email and message, so we can check you run it.

If you turn on notifications: a code from your phone that lets us send them.

If you use a friend's invite code: which account invited you, so they can earn a badge. They only see how many friends joined, not who.

Your location

If you allow it, your phone's location is used to sort places by distance and show what's near you. This happens on your phone, and only while you're using the app.

When you look at posts "near me" or nearby places, an approximate position is sent with that request so the right results come back. We don't keep a history of where you've been, and your location is never shown to other people.

If you turn on "New places near you" alerts, we save your area, rounded to about 1 km, so we can tell you when a halal place opens nearby. Turning the alerts off deletes it.

You can switch location off at any time in your phone's settings. The app still works: search by town or postcode instead.

Why we use it (our lawful basis)

To run your account and show what you post: this is needed to provide the app you signed up for (contract).

To keep Hunt Halal safe and follow the law, including the Online Safety Act: checking photos and posts, handling reports and appeals, stopping spam and fake reviews, giving under-18s safer settings and suspending accounts that break the rules (legal obligation and our legitimate interest in a safe, honest community).

To recommend places and posts you'll probably like (our legitimate interest; you can change your food picks in Settings → Your tastes).

To understand how Hunt Halal is used and improve it, for example finding places people search for that are missing (our legitimate interest). UK law allows these anonymous statistics without asking first, as long as we tell you and you can easily say no, which you can in Settings.

To show a business owner totals for their own listing, such as how many people viewed or saved it in the last 30 days. Owners never see who.

Location, notifications, camera and photos are only used if you allow them, and you can switch them off in your phone's settings.

We don't sell your information, we don't show adverts, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Automatic checks

Photos and video covers are checked automatically by an image-checking service (run by one of our service providers) before they appear, to stop rude or unsafe pictures. Text is checked against a list of offensive words. Pictures the checker isn't sure about are looked at by a person.

If something of yours is removed or your account is suspended, we tell you in Notifications. You can ask for a person to look at the decision again: tap the notification, or go to Settings → Send feedback → Appeal a decision.

Who helps us run the app

A cloud database provider: stores the database and accounts.

Apple and Google: if you choose to continue with them, they confirm who you are. We never see your Apple or Google password.

A cloud storage provider: stores photos and videos, and runs the automatic photo check.

A notifications service: delivers notifications to your phone.

Open map services: show the map. Your phone asks them for map pictures, like any map website.

Code delivery services: deliver the map's code to your phone only if it isn't already built into the app.

A postcode lookup service: when you search for a town or postcode, what you typed is sent to it to find where that is on the map.

These companies only use your information to provide their service to us. Some are based outside the UK; where that's the case, the transfer is protected by the safeguards UK law requires (such as the UK's approved contract terms or the UK-US data bridge).

What you post publicly (reviews, comments, photos, public lists, your display name, profile photo and Hunt Score) can be seen by anyone using Hunt Halal. People who follow you may see that you've reviewed a place they're looking at. Anyone can see who liked a post, and how many people liked a comment. Lists you make private are only visible to you.

We may share information with the police or other authorities if the law requires it, or to protect someone from serious harm.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Settings → Account Settings → Delete account removes your account, reviews, comments, messages you sent, feedback and your photos straight away.

Usage data is deleted after 13 months.

Exceptions: business logos you added stay on the business's page; records of reports, removals, suspensions and edit history are kept as long as needed to keep the app safe and show we follow the law; copies in backups disappear within a few weeks.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, get a copy in a common format, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or object to how we use it. Email contact@hunthalal.com. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk.

Under-18s

Hunt Halal is for people aged 13 and over. If you're under 13, please don't make an account. If you think a child under 13 has an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

If you're 13 to 17: only people you follow can start a chat with you, you aren't suggested to people you don't know, usage data starts switched off, and your location is never shown to anyone. You can block and report anyone, any time. If something online worries you, tell an adult you trust, or contact Childline (0800 1111, childline.org.uk).

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app if the change is important.
