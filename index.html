NO SMOKE, NO MIRRORS

Why you can trust us.

Anyone can slap "halal" on a listing. We'd rather show you Unverified than pretend to know something we don't. Here is exactly how Hunt Halal works.

We show the evidence

Every listing carries one of four badges, so you always know how its halal status is known and how sure we are.

Real places, open sources

Listings come from OpenStreetMap, the Food Standards Agency's business register and Overture Maps, plus places added by the community.

A person checks it

Every place you suggest is reviewed before it goes live, and every reported post is looked at by a real person.

Look before you sign up

Browse everything as a guest. You only need an account when you want to review, save favourites or earn Hunt Score.

Our honest small print: halal information can change. Chains switch suppliers and certificates expire. If it matters to you, always check with the business. We'll always tell you how much we know.
