Who can use Hunt Halal

You must be 13 or older. We ask your age group once; under-18s get safer settings (see the Privacy Policy). Keep your password private: you're responsible for what happens on your account.

Halal information

Hunt Halal is not a halal certification body. Halal information comes from map data, businesses and the community, and is labelled with how it was confirmed (Certified, Owner confirmed, Community or Unverified).

It can be wrong or out of date. If halal status matters to you, always confirm with the business, for example by asking to see their certificate.

Rules for what you post

Be honest: only review places you've actually been to, and say what really happened. Don't post a review you were paid or given anything for (see How reviews work, below).

No hate, harassment, bullying or threats, including about anyone's religion, race, gender or anything else about who they are.

No nudity, sexual content, violence or gore, nothing that encourages suicide, self-harm or eating disorders, and nothing illegal.

No spam, fake reviews, or reviews of your own business or a competitor's.

Don't post other people's private information, or photos of people who haven't agreed.

Only post photos and videos you took or have permission to use.

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. We remove content that breaks these rules and can warn, suspend or permanently ban accounts.

How we keep people safe

We check photos automatically before they appear, filter offensive words, limit how fast new accounts can post and message, look at every report, and give under-18s safer settings. We remove illegal content as soon as we know about it, and report it to the authorities where the law requires.

Reporting and blocking

You can report any post, comment, photo, list, message or place with the flag or ⋯ menu, and block anyone from their profile or a chat. We review reports, normally within 24 hours, and act on content that breaks the rules or the law.

Businesses

Reviews: Our fake review policy

Every review on Hunt Halal must be someone's own honest experience of a place they've been to. UK law (the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024) bans fake reviews, and so do we.

Not allowed: reviews of places you haven't been to; reviews written by, for or against a business by its owner, staff, family or competitors; reviews you were paid for or given anything for (money, free food, discounts, vouchers or anything else); swapping reviews; and using more than one account.

Hunt Halal doesn't allow reviews given in return for anything at all, so if a business offers you something for a review, please don't post it, and tell us.

Reviews: How we stop fake reviews

You need an account to review. Business owners can't review a place they run, and can't edit, hide or remove customers' reviews.

You can post a new review of the same place at most once every 3 days, and there are limits on how many reviews one account can post in an hour and a day.

Reviews from accounts less than 14 days old count for less in a place's star rating (see below). Several reviews of one place from new accounts in a short time are flagged to our team automatically.

Text is checked for offensive words and spam, photos are checked automatically, and anyone can report a review (⋯ → Report → Fake review). A person looks at every report, normally within 24 hours.

Reviews: How star ratings are worked out

A place's rating is the average of each person's latest review of it, so one person counts once.

For their first 14 days, reviews from new accounts count at 30% in the average. They still show in full and count in the number of reviews. After 14 days they count fully and the rating updates by itself.

When we remove a review, the rating and the number of reviews are recalculated straight away.

Reviews: What happens to fake reviews

We remove them. Each removal is a strike: 3 strikes in 30 days means a 7-day suspension, and serious or repeated fake reviews mean a permanent ban.

If a business is behind fake reviews, we can remove its ability to manage its listing, and we may tell the Competition and Markets Authority.

Reviews: Hunt Score points

You earn Hunt Score points for reviewing, adding photos and checking halal details. You get the same points whatever star rating you give. Points have no money value and can't be swapped for anything.

Reviews: Reviews and complaints

A review tells other people about your experience. If you want something put right by the business (a refund, say), contact the business directly: we can't sort it out for you.

We don't remove genuine reviews because they're negative, and businesses can't pay to hide or remove reviews. If you run a business and think a review is fake, untrue or defamatory, report it or email us and we'll look at it, and may ask the reviewer about it.
